FAQ

Everything you want to know

When is fresh fig season?

Harvest season in the Asir highlands starts in early summer and runs to its end. Production is underway now, with daily hand-picking at perfect ripeness. Message us for today's availability.

How do I order from Naderia Farm?

Directly via WhatsApp on +966 50 318 4880 — or the "Order on WhatsApp" button on any product. We reply as fast as we can.

Is delivery available?

We arrange delivery and shipping based on your location and order size — message us on WhatsApp and we'll sort out the best option.

Do you supply wholesale quantities?

Yes — we supply shops and events with suitable quantities. Contact us for details.

When are saplings and cuttings available?

Saplings are available to order and reserve; cuttings follow the pruning season. Message us and we'll guide you to what suits your region and planting time.

Can I visit the farm?

You are most welcome — visits are available by prior arrangement via WhatsApp.
