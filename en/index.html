Our story

We come from this land,
and it is part of us.

In Al Far’ayn, among the highlands of Ahad Rafidah, our story grows alongside the fig tree. We care for each tree from its first leaf to its last fruit, and we believe good fruit begins with a farmer’s patience and love for the land.

Naderia is a farm, a nursery, and a place to share what we love: good fruit, plants that grow with you, and a little of the spirit of the south.

Before you ask

Good questions,
clear answers.

Still have something on your mind? We are here and happy to talk.

Ask us on WhatsApp
How do I place an order?

Add your favourite products to your selection, review the quantities, then continue on WhatsApp. We go over availability, price, weight or sapling size, and collection or delivery with you before anything is confirmed.

Do I pay anything on this site?

No. The site takes no payment and needs no account. The farm team confirms the price and order details with you directly on WhatsApp.

Are fresh figs available all year?

Fresh figs are seasonal, and availability depends on ripeness and harvesting. Message us to find out what is available now, or ask about dried figs and fig paste.

Can you deliver to my area?

Delivery and shipping are arranged according to your location, product and quantity. Add your city before you send your selection, so we can explain the options and cost before anything is confirmed.

Can I visit the farm?

You are very welcome. Visits are by prior arrangement; contact us to agree on a time and receive the exact farm location.

Which sapling should I choose?

Tell us your region and whether you plan to grow in the ground or a pot. We will help you choose a suitable sapling or cutting and talk you through its care when you order.

Do you take wholesale and event orders?

Yes. Get in touch to arrange quantities for shops, events and gifts. Share an approximate quantity and date, and we will confirm what we can provide.

Are the images of your actual products?

The current images are illustrative, not photographs of the farm’s products or packaging. Ask us on WhatsApp if you would like to see what is available today.
