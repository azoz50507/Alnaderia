About us

A story of land — and family

From the heart of Asir, a region pulsing with beauty and heritage — and precisely from the historic valley of Al-Far'ayn in Ahad Rafidah — Naderia Farm and Nurseries was founded by Abdullah Ayedh Abu Darman Al-Qahtani "Abu Abdulkarim".

We don't simply offer farm produce; we tell a story of passion for the land and a deep bond with the authentic identity of the Saudi South — from terraced fields embracing the mountain peaks, to an elegant box arriving at your door.

"We grow with love and harvest with care, to bring you the finest our generous land has to give."

Land of the Two Valleys 🏞️

Al-Far'ayn takes its name from the two valleys that cradle its villages: Wadi Al-Jurm, winding through the villages of Al Nader — the place that inspired the name "Naderia" — and Wadi Al-Sarar. They meet at Al-Mafsah, where terraced farms have flourished for centuries.
