Your privacy, explained.

Here is how the website works: what stays in your browser and when your order details move to WhatsApp.

Your selections on your device

The website saves selected product types and quantities in your browser's local storage, so you can return to them on the same device and browser. This list is not automatically sent to the farm.

You can edit or clear the list in the order panel. You can also remove all saved data using your browser's site-data settings.

Contacting us and sending your request

Contact details entered in the order form remain in the page's memory while you use it; the website does not save them in local storage. Choosing to continue to WhatsApp adds your request details to a prepared message in a WhatsApp link.

The farm receives the message when you send it yourself in WhatsApp. Opening the link makes its included information subject to that external service's processing and policies. If you contact us, we use the information you share to answer your enquiry and arrange your request.

Website visits and external services

The current pages do not load analytics, advertising or tracking tools. Website fonts and images are served from this site's own files.

The hosting service may process routine technical data, such as network addresses and request times, to deliver pages and operate the service. Opening WhatsApp or maps takes you to an external service with its own privacy policy.
