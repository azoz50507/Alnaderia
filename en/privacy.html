Naderia · In plain words

Your privacy, explained.

Here is how the website works: what stays in your browser and when your selection moves to WhatsApp.

Your selection on your device

The website saves selected product types and quantities in your browser’s local storage, so you can return to them on the same device and browser. Your selection is not sent to the farm automatically.

You can edit or clear your selection in the selection panel. You can also remove all saved data in your browser’s site data settings.

Contacting us and sending your selection

Contact details entered in the selection panel remain in the page’s memory while you use it; the website does not save them in local storage. Choosing to continue to WhatsApp adds your selection to a prepared message in a WhatsApp link.

The farm receives the message when you send it yourself in WhatsApp. Once you open the link, the information in it is handled under WhatsApp’s own processing and policies. If you contact us, we use the information you share to answer your enquiry and arrange your order.

Website visits and external services

The current pages do not load analytics, advertising or tracking tools. Website fonts and images are served from this site’s own files.

The hosting service may process routine technical data, such as network addresses and request times, to deliver pages and operate the service. Opening WhatsApp takes you to an external service with its own privacy policy.
