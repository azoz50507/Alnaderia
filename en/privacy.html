Privacy Policy

Naderia Farm & Nurseries — alnaderia.com

Your privacy matters to us. This page explains, briefly and transparently, what we collect when you visit the site and how we use it — in line with Saudi Arabia's Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL).

What we collect

We use Google Analytics to collect anonymous usage data only, which helps us improve the site and serve you better, including:

  • Which pages and sections are visited, and for how long.
  • Button clicks (such as "Order on WhatsApp") and which products draw the most interest.
  • Device type (mobile/desktop), traffic source (search, WhatsApp, direct link) and general location (city/country).

What we do NOT collect

We do not collect any personal data that identifies you: not your name, phone number, or email — unless you contact us yourself on WhatsApp, in which case we use your details only to answer and serve your request.

Consent & cookies

Analytics are enabled only after your explicit consent via the banner shown on your first visit. If you choose "Decline", no data is collected. These tools use lightweight cookies for measurement purposes only.

Your rights

You may decline tracking at any time (by clearing your browser data, or enabling "Do Not Track" in your browser). You can also contact us with any privacy question.
